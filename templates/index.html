    🛡️ Smart Purchase Duplicate Protection (Zero Double-Stock Risk):
    • Accidental Re-save Hard Block: Agar wahi purchase bill galti se dobara scan ya save kiya jaye, to BillZen use turant block kar dega! Na duplicate stock badhega, na galat Input Tax Credit (ITC) add hoga.
    • Genuine Part-Bills Supported: Agar ek hi supplier se same invoice number ke 2 genuine alag part-bills ya alag items aate hain, to BillZen unhe safely allow karta hai.
    • OCR Variation Tolerance: Dobara scan karne par agar rate ya quantity mein minor OCR scan variation ho, tab bhi duplicate pehchaan kar dukan ka stock aur accounting safe rakhta hai.
    • Zero Waste: Duplicate attempt par database, stock aur GST mein 0% change hota hai.
    💡 Supplier profile automatically GSTIN, phone aur email ke saath directory mein save ho jati hai.
    🔄 Returns & Credit / Debit Notes — Stock & Ledger Auto-Sync
    Sales Return (Credit Note — Bikri Vapasi):
    • Customer maal wapas kare to Sales History ya Returns page se Credit Note banayein.
    • Wapas aaya maal automatically shop inventory stock mein add ho jata hai.
    • Customer ka khata balance kam hota hai aur Output GST liability automatically reverse ho jati hai.

    Purchase Return (Debit Note — Kharid Vapasi):
    • Supplier ko defective ya excess maal wapas bhejne par Debit Note banayein.
    • Maal ka stock automatically minus hota hai (negative stock prevention ke saath).
    • Supplier balance aur Input Tax Credit (ITC) real-time adjust hota hai.

    💡 100% Mathematically Synced: Returns banate hi Inventory stock, Customer/Supplier Khata aur GST balance real-time update ho jate hain.

    ⚖️ GST Rule 46 & Section 170 Rounding (₹0.01 Explanation):
    Indian GST Rules (CGST Rule 46) ke tehat tax har line-item par alag calculate hokar standard 2 decimal places tak round hota hai. Sabhi items ke rounded taxes ka sum, bill subtotal ke bulk tax percentage se kabhi-kabhi ₹0.01 aage-peeche ho sakta hai. Ye 100% legal, statutory compliant aur natural standard rounding hai (CGST Act Section 170). Tally Prime aur GST Portal dono ise standard accounting practice ke roop mein accept karte hain.
